# Music credits

All six tracks were composed and recorded by Shurron Green for Pink Slips in 2026 and are part of this repository under its terms.

| File | Length | Where it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| track1.mp3 | 1:00 | In the shuffle |
| track2.mp3 to track6.mp3 | 2:00 each | In the shuffle |

All six play in a shuffled order across the start screen and matches, quieter during a match, with a new track on every change of screen and no track repeated back to back.

The originals are 48 kHz stereo WAV files kept by the owner. `scripts/audio/encode.py` produces these MP3 copies.
